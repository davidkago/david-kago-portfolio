Making conversational AI work for customers.

I worked across product, business analysis, and delivery to help improve conversational AI experiences. My work connected customer needs with technical behavior, testing, and production performance.

RequirementsTurn business needs into clear, testable expectations.
ValidationUse test scenarios to check expected behavior.

The challenge

Conversational AI needs to do more than recognize a customer's words. It needs to respond appropriately, handle unexpected behavior, and provide a useful path forward.

That takes clear requirements, realistic test scenarios, and a way to investigate issues that appear in production.

What I contributed

  • Helped translate business needs into user stories and acceptance criteria.
  • Worked on test scenarios to check whether conversational AI behaved as expected.
  • Helped investigate production issues and coordinate follow-through across business and technical teams.

How I approached it

I connected three things: what the customer needed, what the system should do, and how the team could verify the result.
